# Skout Plan

## Product Direction

Build a fast, read-only fantasy baseball advisor that combines a public Yahoo league with trustworthy MLB and analytical context, keeps complete usable data locally, and presents decisions clearly in the terminal.

## Ideas To Explore

- IE1: Add daily and weekly head-to-head matchup views.
- IE2: Add fantasy roster and category-total views.
- IE3: Add hitter and pitcher pools, waiver filters, and detail cards.
- IE4: Add an explicitly confirmed local-state reset command.
